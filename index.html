The gap we close

Wanting to shop Black-owned is easy. Knowing who to trust is hard.

Directories list everyone. Deal apps chase strangers with the deepest discount. Neither tells you which brands are genuinely excellent, the ones worth coming back to.

Woven is built to close that gap. Think of us as the Michelin Guide for Black-owned brands: not the biggest list, the most trusted one.

A directory tells you who exists. A standard tells you who's worth it.

The Standard

One Mark. Earned, Never Bought.

This is the seal a Woven Certified brand earns so consumers know that it's been vetted.

  • 01Product Quality & CraftWe try the products ourselves and check references before a brand earns the mark.
  • 02Brand Story & FounderWe connect with brand founders to establish relationships rooted in care and the village mindset.
  • 03Values & Community ImpactWe look at how a brand shows up for community, not only at what it sells.
  • 04Customer ExperienceWe confirm the brand is ready for you to engage with their product and able to deliver.
