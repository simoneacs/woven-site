Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Woven ("Woven," "we," "us," or "our"), a service operated by Anansi Creative Studio LLC, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit wovenvillage.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Information you give us directly. When you join our mailing list, apply for certification, ask a question, or RSVP to an event, you may provide details such as your name, email address, business name, and anything you choose to include in a message or form.

Information collected automatically. We use privacy-focused analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that measures aggregate traffic, such as page views and referring sites, without cookies and without building a profile of you. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs.

Local storage. We use your browser's local storage to remember that you have already seen the Site's intro animation. This is stored on your device and is not personal information.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To send you updates and announcements you have asked to receive.
  • To review certification applications and manage event RSVPs.
  • To understand and improve how the Site is used.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate, and only as needed for them to perform their function. These currently include email delivery (Sender), forms (Tally), event RSVPs (Posh), website analytics (Cloudflare), website hosting (GitHub Pages), and font delivery (Google Fonts). Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Woven, our members, or others.

Cookies and tracking

The Site itself does not use tracking cookies, and our analytics are cookieless. Third-party tools you interact with (for example, when you open our RSVP or application forms) may set their own cookies, governed by their policies.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any message. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above, and significant changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact us
